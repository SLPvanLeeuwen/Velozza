Velozza Press & Booking Kit

Raw, high-energy thrash rooted in the spirit of the '80s.

Formed in 2016 in Roermond, NL, Velozza delivers uncompromising thrash metal. Having shared the stage with international acts such as Sacred Reich, D.R.I., Angelus Apatrida, Gama Bomb, Lich King, and Chemicide, they know how to command a crowd. Fast, tight, and relentless — Velozza brings a full-power show built for headbanging crowds.

For fans of: Exodus • Kreator • Slayer • Havok
