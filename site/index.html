About Velozza NWOTM • Roermond, NL

Forged in old-school thrash, sharpened by modern aggression.

VELOZZA delivers fast, riff-driven metal rooted in the spirit of Exodus, Kreator, and Slayer. Pushing forward as part of the New Wave Of Thrash Metal (NWOTM), they bring a high-octane live show designed specifically to ignite headbanging crowds and moshpits.

A Live Act Built on Experience & Hunger

A well-seasoned rhythm section in their early 50s, a vocalist/guitarist in his 30s, and a lead guitarist in his 20s — all sharing the same drive, intensity, and uncompromising thrash mentality. This mix of experience and hunger translates directly into tight, high-energy live shows with a rare balance of precision and raw aggression. No gimmicks, no compromises—just pure thrash metal with conviction and intent.

No gimmicks, no compromises. VELOZZA’s live sets combine extreme tempos (210–220 BPM) with heavy, groove-driven mid-tempo crushers around 145 BPM, guaranteeing maximum dynamic impact and moshpit response.

Following Apocaholic (2021) and Bankrupt the Republic (2023), the band returns with their strongest statement to date: Humanity (2026), a fully self-produced album earning critical acclaim recognition across the international metal press.

Live Credibility: Shared the stage with prominent acts including Sacred Reich, Angelus Apatrida, Gama Bomb, Lich King, D.R.I., and Chemicide.
