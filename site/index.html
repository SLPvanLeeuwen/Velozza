Forged in old-school thrash, sharpened by modern aggression.

Hailing from Roermond - the Netherlands, VELOZZA delivers fast, riff-driven metal rooted in the spirit of Exodus, Kreator, and Slayer, while pushing forward as part of the NWOTM movement (New Wave Of Thrash Metal).

What sets the band apart is its lineup spanning generations:

A well-seasoned rhythm section in their early 50s, a vocalist/guitarist in his 30s, and a lead guitarist in his 20s — all sharing the same drive, intensity, and uncompromising thrash mentality. This mix of experience and hunger translates directly into tight, high-energy live shows with a rare balance of precision and raw aggression.

No gimmicks, no compromises—just pure thrash metal with conviction and intent. VELOZZA’s sound combines extreme tempos (up to 210–220 BPM) with heavy, groove-driven mid-tempo crushers around 145 BPM, creating dynamic sets built for maximum impact and moshpit response.

Following Apocaholic (2021) and Bankrupt the Republic (2023), the band returns with their strongest statement to date: Humanity (2026), a fully self-recorded and self-produced album. The release received strong critical recognition across the international metal press, including:

  • Aardschok (85/100)
  • Metalfan.nl (79/100)
  • Metallurg (95/100)
  • Headliner (79/100)

VELOZZA has shared the stage with prominent acts like Sacred Reich, Angelus Apatrida, Gama Bomb, Lich King, D.R.I., Struck a Nerve, Chemicide, Martyr, and Mortal Sin.
